Markloom

A free, open-source Markdown editor with live preview, Mermaid diagrams and clean export to Word, PDF and HTML. It runs in your browser or as a desktop app, and your documents stay on your computer.

  • Split view (source and live preview) or a rich-text editor that keeps untouched Markdown as it was
  • Mermaid diagrams that render as you type, with errors pointing at the line
  • Export to Word (.docx), PDF, standalone HTML and portable Markdown
  • Copy as rich text into email, Google Docs or Confluence
  • Tabs, folder tree, quick open, search across files, version history
