# Code of conduct

Markloom is a small project, and we want it to be a friendly place to ask questions, report bugs and contribute.

## Our standards

Please:

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to open source.
- Assume good intent, and ask before you assume.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person, and accept it the same way.
- Respect that people have different experience, backgrounds and time to give.

Not acceptable:

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome attention.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Anything else a reasonable person would find inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in every project space: issues, pull requests, discussions, the wiki, and anywhere you represent the project.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour worries you, report it privately to the maintainer through their [GitHub profile](https://github.com/boudou). Reports are kept confidential. The maintainer may edit or remove contributions, or block anyone who breaks this code of conduct.
